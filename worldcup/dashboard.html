How the model works

1. Live state. The 16 remaining teams and the actual fixed knockout bracket as of 2026-07-04 (Round of 16). Live knockout projection — simulates forward from the actual Round-of-16 bracket. Germany & the Netherlands were eliminated in the Round of 32; Argentina survived Cape Verde 3-2 AET.

2. Learned goal engine. A scikit-learn PoissonRegressor predicts goals scored from attacking rating, defending rating and home advantage, with learned coefficients attack +0.424, defence -0.418, home +0.251 — hosts (USA, Mexico, Canada) get a real edge on home soil.

3. Monte-Carlo knockout. The bracket is played out 50,000 times — every tie decided by the model, with extra time and penalties on draws. Each team's share of simulated titles is its probability of winning.

Strength ratings reflect current form and market consensus; re-run model.py after each round to refresh every figure on this page.
