How the model works

1. Strength ratings. Every team carries an Elo rating that distils real historical results into a single number — the higher the gap, the more one side is expected to dominate.

2. Learned goal engine. A scikit-learn PoissonRegressor is trained to predict goals scored from the attacking and defending ratings plus home advantage. It recovered coefficients attack +0.424, defence -0.418, home +0.251 — i.e. stronger attack and weaker opposing defence both raise expected goals, and hosts get a real edge.

3. Monte-Carlo tournament. The model plays every group match and knockout tie (extra time and penalties included) across 20,000 full tournaments. Counting how often each team lifts the trophy gives the probabilities above.

The field, group draw and ratings are a reproducible model projection, not the official FIFA draw — swap in official numbers and re-run model.py to update every figure on this page.
